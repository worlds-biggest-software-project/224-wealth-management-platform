## Legal & IP Summary

Wealth management platforms operate under strict regulatory frameworks: MiFID II (EU) mandates client classification, product suitability documentation, transaction reporting, and 5-year record retention in WORM format; Reg BI (US) requires advisors to document best-interest compliance and disclose conflicts. GDPR and CCPA govern client data privacy. GIPS standards (CFA Institute) are expected by institutional clients for performance reporting. DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act, EU, effective 2025) requires platforms to demonstrate third-party vendor risk controls and incident response capabilities. All proprietary platforms (Advyzon, Orion, Salesforce, etc.) own their ML models for suitability and rebalancing; users are responsible for ensuring documentation and auditability. No open-source compliance risks; all major platforms are proprietary with commercial support contracts.
